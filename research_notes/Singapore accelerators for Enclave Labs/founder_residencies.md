## Q1. Which programs exist and are live in Singapore (inventory), and which are stale or dead?

### Takeaway
The live Singapore residency and accelerator options for Enclave are Antler Singapore (revamped 6-week residency), Iterative (YC-style, SEA-focused), Peak XV Surge (rolling, SEA-eligible), Tribe's Ignition AI Accelerator (NVIDIA-stack, needs Singapore incorporation), and the Microsoft/EnterpriseSG/NUS BLOCK71 "AI Accelerate" track. Entrepreneur First Singapore is closed: it shut in 2023 and has not reopened. Hustle Fund's Asia terms are stale. No Singapore-specific "AI residency" launched in 2025–2026 could be verified.

### Cited Findings
**Entrepreneur First (EF) Singapore: closed**
- EF closed its Berlin, Canadian and Singapore offices in spring 2023 — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Entrepreneurs_First)
- DealStreetAsia: "Entrepreneur First pulls the plug on SG and Toronto amid global restructuring". EF is consolidating on Europe, North America and India — [DealStreetAsia](https://www.dealstreetasia.com/stories/entrepreneur-first-to-cease-sg-toronto-programmes-314319)
- Sifted reported that EF would pull out of Toronto, Berlin and Singapore while launching in New York, and would keep running London, Paris and Bangalore — [Sifted](https://sifted.eu/articles/entrepreneur-first-launches-in-the-us-news)
- A 2026 guide lists EF cohort locations as London, Paris, Bangalore and San Francisco, with no Singapore. It says applications are individual: you apply solo, without a co-founder or idea. On the US pathway, EF invests up to $250K: a $125K post-money SAFE for 8% plus a $125K uncapped MFN SAFE, once a Delaware C-Corp is formed — [Causo guide (third-party)](https://hub.causo.ai/guides/how-to-apply-to-entrepreneur-first-2026)
- Historical only: EF Singapore launched in 2016 with S$50K per startup — [GovInsider](https://govinsider.asia/intl-en/article/singapore-brings-uk-model-to-build-tech-startups). Some directory listings still show an active SG program, but they are STALE — [incubatorlist](https://incubatorlist.com/entrepreneur-first-singapore)

**Antler Singapore: active**
- January 2026 announcement: "improved investment terms and a revamped 6-week residency for founders building global software companies" — [Antler blog](https://www.antler.co/blog/antler-singapore-announces-improved-investment-terms-and-6-week-residency)
- The residency is full-time and in person in Singapore. Founders must meet visa eligibility for their location — [Antler apply page](https://www.antler.co/apply). An older listing says Antler SG can help with visas — [runfutureproof (aggregator)](https://www.runfutureproof.com/acceleratorhub/programs/antler-residency-singapore)
- Antler says there is no fee to participate and that it invests in fewer than 2% of applicants. It has invested in 1,600+ companies globally (e.g., Airalo, Lovable) since launching in Singapore in 2018 — [Antler Singapore page](https://www.antler.co/location/singapore)
- Antler Elevate is not a residency. It is a US$285M emerging-growth fund (Series A+) managed from London, Singapore and New York. It also invests in non-Antler startups that raised seed elsewhere — [Antler blog (June 2023)](https://antler.co/blog/antler-elevate-emerging-growth-fund-close); [TechNode Global](https://technode.global/2023/06/08/antler-closes-285m-emerging-growth-fund-to-back-industry-leaders/)

**Iterative: active**
- Singapore-based YC-style accelerator focused exclusively on SEA. It runs two batches a year: a 3-month program ending in a Demo Day — [TechCrunch 2021](https://techcrunch.com/2021/12/06/as-southeast-asia-start-to-boom-an-accelerator-backed-by-silicon-valley-execs-says-its-a-way-in); [Iterative demo day page](https://www.iterative.vc/demoday)
- S26 is its 13th batch, with Demo Day on Oct 14, 2026. It drew more than 1,000 applications. Focus areas are AI, fintech and the creator economy — [Iterative demo day page](https://www.iterative.vc/demoday)

**Peak XV Surge: active, global and SEA-eligible**
- The Surge 12 cohort (18 startups) was announced in late Sept 2026. The seed ceiling rose to $5M from $3M. Surge has backed 180+ startups since 2019 — [TechCrunch, 2026-09-28](https://techcrunch.com/2026/09/28/peak-xv-goes-bigger-at-seed-with-new-surge-cohort-as-series-a-bar-rises/); [YourStory](https://yourstory.com/2026/09/peak-xv-selects-18-startups-12th-surge-cohort-doubles-down-ai-deep-tech)
- Surge covers India, SEA, the US, Singapore and the UAE, investing $500K–$5M at seed — [aichief summary of TechCrunch](https://aichief.com/news/peak-xv-ups-surge-seed-investment-ceiling-to-5m-unveils-18-startup-cohort/)
- Peak XV hosted a Singapore Surge open house (invite-only) — [Luma](https://luma.com/6a04otg4)

**Tribe / Ignition AI Accelerator: active, AI-specific**
- Run by Tribe with NVIDIA Inception and support from Digital Industry Singapore (DISG). It was established in May 2024 — [EDB media release](https://www.edb.gov.sg/en/about-edb/media-releases-publications/new-ai-accelerator-launches-in-singapore-with-industry-support-from-tribe-disg-and-nvidia.html); [Ignition site](https://theignition.ai/)
- The 2026/2027 batch will take 18 companies over 12 months — [Ignition site](https://theignition.ai/)

**Microsoft x EnterpriseSG x NUS Enterprise "AI Accelerate" (BLOCK71): active**
- Announced 29 Oct 2025. Up to 150 AI startups get fast-tracked access to the Startup SG Tech grant over 3 years, via DISG. The program builds on AI Accelerate, a 10-week BLOCK71 incubator built on Azure, and adds a go-to-market track — [EnterpriseSG release](https://www.enterprisesg.gov.sg/resources/media-centre/media-releases/2025/october/mr04825_microsoft-enterprise-singapore-and-nus-enterprise-to-fast-track-growth-for-150-ai-startups); [CIO World Asia](https://cioworldasia.com/2025/10/30/microsoft-enterprise-singapore-and-nus-enterprise-to-fast-track-growth-for-150-ai-startups/)

**Google for Startups Accelerator**
- The AI First (Singapore) page shows applications closed, with the last deadline in Feb 2025 — [Google](https://startup.google.com/programs/accelerator/ai-first/singapore/)
- The SEA accelerator is equity-free and runs 3 months. Its cohort of 25 started Aug 2026 — [Google SEA](https://startup.google.com/programs/accelerator/southeast-asia/)

**Accel Atoms**
- Atoms gives $250K in pre-seed capital via a convertible note. It is aimed at Indian and Indian-origin founders "building from anywhere, including India and Southeast Asia" — [startupgrantsindia (aggregator)](https://www.startupgrantsindia.com/providers/accel-atoms). An earlier cohort explicitly opened to SEA startups — [Telangana Today](https://telanganatoday.com/tag/atoms-programme). An "Atoms AI Cohort 2026" ran with Google's AI Futures Fund — same aggregator; eligibility not verified.

**Hustle Fund**
- Historically wrote ~US$25K checks at MVP stage, followed by a 4–6 week growth project — [e27 (2020)](https://e27.co/hustle-fund-ii-hits-first-close-at-us30m-to-invest-in-pre-seed-startups-in-sea-us-20201105/); [Shanda release](https://www.shanda.com/?p=1629). Fund II had a $33.6M final close — [DealStreetAsia](https://dealstreetasia.com/stories/hustle-fund-final-close-228838). These figures are STALE: there is no 2026 source.

**Government and deep-tech support (not residencies)**
- SGInnovate is a government-owned deep-tech platform that co-invests in research-driven startups — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/SGInnovate). It runs "Summation", a deep-tech talent program — [SGInnovate](https://www.sginnovate.com/apprenticeship/talent). **"AI residency" launched 2025–2026**

### Gaps

- Accel Atoms' eligibility for non-Indian-origin Singapore founders is unconfirmed.

## Q2. Which accept an existing company with a product (vs individuals pre-team)?

### Takeaway
Iterative, Surge, Ignition and AI Accelerate are built for existing companies. Ignition actually requires commercial validation or at least seed funding. Antler's revamped Singapore residency now targets "high-conviction teams" with a validated problem and early MVP, but prior outside equity may affect eligibility. EF is individual-first and also closed in Singapore.

### Cited Findings
- Antler Singapore's January 2026 Inception Residency is "designed for high-conviction teams and proven founders with a validated problem and an early MVP". Its Singapore page targets founding teams ready to "condense 6 months of work into 6 weeks" — [Antler blog](https://www.antler.co/blog/antler-singapore-announces-improved-investment-terms-and-6-week-residency); [Antler Singapore](https://www.antler.co/location/singapore)
- Antler MENAP says a team "with a validated concept and early traction" can join. It also says that if your company has already raised equity from external investors, "it may affect your eligibility". Antler says its terms are consistent across locations, but this has not been confirmed for SG — [Antler MENAP](https://www.antler.co/residency/menap)
- Iterative backs startups at the idea or early stage with full funding at admission. It "invests the full amount upon admission" — [Caplight / altss profiles summarised](https://altss.com/profile/iterative.md)
- Surge says founders can apply "at any stage", with rolling review — [Surge FAQ (via search extract)](https://surge.peakxv.com/faqs)
- Ignition AI Accelerator eligibility:
  - Incorporated for 10 years or less.
  - Singapore-based, or committed to incorporating in Singapore by the end of the program.
  - Built on an NVIDIA stack, or committed to adopting it within 3 months.
  - Commercially validated (paying customers or signed pilots) or funded, minimally at seed stage.
  - Source: [Ignition site](https://theignition.ai/)
- Startup SG Founder requires a Singapore citizen or PR co-founder — [deeptech.sg](https://www.deeptech.sg/grants.php)
- Most Singapore grants require Singapore incorporation and local operational presence — [karman.com.sg (vendor)](https://karman.com.sg/blog/ai-startups-incorporating-singapore-2026)
- EF's 2026 application is solo or individual, with no co-founder or idea required — [Causo guide](https://hub.causo.ai/guides/how-to-apply-to-entrepreneur-first-2026)

### Inferences
- For Enclave, an existing co-founded team with a product, the best fits are:
  1. Iterative.
  2. Peak XV Surge.
  3. Ignition, if Enclave is willing to incorporate in Singapore and fit the NVIDIA stack. An in-VPC GPU inference story could plausibly qualify.
  4. Antler SG, if Enclave hasn't raised priced outside equity.
- Enclave's sovereign, in-VPC positioning maps naturally onto Ignition's NVIDIA/DISG enterprise orientation.

### Gaps
- Not verified: whether Iterative and Surge require Singapore incorporation, or let a non-Singapore holding company (e.g., Delaware) participate. Iterative's SEA focus implies the company must serve or be based in SEA.
- Antler SG's exact rules on prior equity raises and existing incorporations were not found on a Singapore-specific page.

## Q4. What equity/cash terms apply?

### Takeaway
- Antler SG: $150K net first ticket ($100K for 10% + $50K uncapped MFN SAFE), with up to $400K total including matching.
- Iterative: $150K–$500K post-money SAFE for about 10–15%.
- Surge: $500K–$5M seed equity.
- Ignition, Google and AI Accelerate are mainly non-dilutive, providing perks or grants.

### Cited Findings
- **Antler SG (Jan 2026):**
  - $100K for 10% via SAFE, plus $50K via an uncapped SAFE with an MFN clause, giving a $150K net first ticket.
  - Up to $250K additional matching capital, tied to 50% of the funding raised from other institutional investors, for up to $400K from inception.
  - $4M in technical perks (GPU credits; OpenAI, Anthropic, AWS, NVIDIA tools).
  - $650K+ in AI perks from day one.
  - Sources: [Antler blog](https://www.antler.co/blog/antler-singapore-announces-improved-investment-terms-and-6-week-residency); [Antler Singapore](https://www.antler.co/location/singapore); [Antler Disrupt](https://content.antler.co/disrupt)
  - Conflicts: a Luma event says "up to $600,000" — [Luma](https://luma.com/2vn5n7es). Older listings say $125K for 12% or $125K for 10%; these are STALE.
- **Iterative:** a standard deal of US$150K–$500K on a post-money SAFE for about 10–15%, with no program fees, invested in full upon admission — [roundfunded (aggregator)](https://www.roundfunded.com/en/vc/iterative); [Iterative apply page extract](https://www.iterative.vc/apply)
- **Peak XV Surge:**
  - $500K–$5M equity investment per company. The ceiling was raised from $3M in Sept 2026, and Surge 12 is the first cohort under it — [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/09/28/peak-xv-goes-bigger-at-seed-with-new-surge-cohort-as-series-a-bar-rises/); [Startup Fortune](https://startupfortune.com/peak-xv-raises-its-surge-seed-check-ceiling-to-5-million-for-18-new-startups/)

  - The equity % is not public.
- **EF (non-SG, for reference):** $125K post-money SAFE for 8% plus a $125K uncapped MFN SAFE — [Causo](https://hub.causo.ai/guides/how-to-apply-to-entrepreneur-first-2026)
- **Accel Atoms:** $250K via convertible note (the aggregator calls it "non-dilutive", which is questionable for a convertible note) — [startupgrantsindia](https://www.startupgrantsindia.com/providers/accel-atoms)
- **Google for Startups SEA:** equity-free — [Google SEA](https://startup.google.com/programs/accelerator/southeast-asia/)
- **AI Accelerate (Microsoft/EnterpriseSG/NUS):** fast-tracked access to the Startup SG Tech grant, non-dilutive — [EnterpriseSG](https://www.enterprisesg.gov.sg/resources/media-centre/media-releases/2025/october/mr04825_microsoft-enterprise-singapore-and-nus-enterprise-to-fast-track-growth-for-150-ai-startups)

- **Hustle Fund:** historically about $25K — STALE — [e27 2020](https://e27.co/hustle-fund-ii-hits-first-close-at-us30m-to-invest-in-pre-seed-startups-in-sea-us-20201105/)

### Inferences
- On cost of capital, Iterative at the high end of its range ($500K for about 10–15%) is cheaper per dollar than Antler ($100K for 10%).
- Surge is the largest check by far, but it is closer to a seed round than a residency.
- Stacking options:
  - The non-dilutive programs (Ignition, AI Accelerate/Startup SG Tech, Google) can generally be combined with one equity program. Most of them require Singapore incorporation.
  - Startup SG Founder also needs a Singapore citizen or PR co-founder.

## Q5. Which have the strongest enterprise/infra AI track record?

### Takeaway
Peak XV Surge has the most visible enterprise-AI and infrastructure picks in 2026 (Reinforce Labs, hiloop, Alma). Antler has the largest global brand outcomes (Lovable, Airalo) but is software-generalist. Iterative's portfolio skews toward SEA fintech and consumer, with only light AI-infra (e.g., Fuzzy AI). Ignition is AI-specific and enterprise-oriented by design, but has thin public alumni data.

### Cited Findings
- **Surge 12 (Sept 2026) includes:**
  - Reinforce Labs: AI evaluation, red teaming and remediation for enterprises.
  - hiloop: infrastructure to train, test and improve open-weight models at scale.
  - Alma: AI-native personal computing infrastructure, from founders who were at Microsoft Research and were founding engineers at Sarvam AI.
  - Rosella: an AI-native commercial insurance brokerage.
  - Sources: [YourStory](https://yourstory.com/2026/09/peak-xv-selects-18-startups-12th-surge-cohort-doubles-down-ai-deep-tech); [The AI Insider](https://theaiinsider.tech/2026/09/30/peak-xv-raises-surge-seed-cap-to-5m-as-ai-startups-dominate-18-company-cohort/); [Entrackr](https://entrackr.com/snippets/peak-xv-unveils-surge-12-with-18-startups-across-ai-robotics-space-consumer-healthcare-music-and-fintech-12589318)
- **Antler:** 1,600+ companies globally, including Airalo and Lovable — [Antler Singapore](https://www.antler.co/location/singapore)
- **Iterative:**
  - Portfolio includes Fuzzy AI (S23; automates AI deployment on hardware devices), Pallo (AI tutors) and GetMo (AI underwriting) — [Iterative companies](https://www.iterative.vc/companies)
  - S26 includes Moneta (AI credit underwriting agent for banks, Indonesia) — [Iterative demo day](https://www.iterative.vc/demoday)
  - Caplight lists 81 portfolio companies, including Polyborg AI (accelerator lead, Apr 2026) — [Caplight](https://www.caplight.com/investor/iterative-vc)
- **Ignition:** AI-only accelerator with NVIDIA Inception and DISG backing, requiring commercially validated companies — [Ignition](https://theignition.ai/); [EDB](https://www.edb.gov.sg/en/about-edb/media-releases-publications/new-ai-accelerator-launches-in-singapore-with-industry-support-from-tribe-disg-and-nvidia.html)
- **Regional context:** AI infrastructure is the largest funded segment of SEA AI ($4.3B across 56 rounds per Tracxn, inflated by Kling AI's $2.8B). Singapore accounts for effectively all of the region's disclosed native AI funding — [Crowdfund Insider](https://www.crowdfundinsider.com/2026/08/297239-southeast-asia-ai-startup-funding-hits-4-1bn-in-2026/)

### Inferences
- For a B2B sovereign-AI infrastructure company:
  - Surge offers the best enterprise-infra peer set and check size.
  - Ignition offers the most direct NVIDIA and Singapore-government enterprise channel. This matters for regulated-industry, in-VPC buyers.
  - Iterative offers SEA go-to-market and a quick pre-seed check.
  - Antler is a fallback if the team wants a structured residency plus perks (GPU credits).
- The Surge 12 portfolio is mostly India- and US-based. A Singapore-based team would need to confirm fit with the SEA team.

### Gaps
- No Singapore-specific Antler alumni in enterprise or infra AI were identified in search results.
- No named Ignition alumni were found.
- No AI Accelerate (BLOCK71) alumni were found.
- No Surge 12 company was confirmed as Singapore-based.

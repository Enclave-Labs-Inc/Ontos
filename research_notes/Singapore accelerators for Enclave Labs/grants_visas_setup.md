# Singapore Grants, Startup Schemes, Founder Visas and Incorporation for Enclave Labs (as of 2026-10-08)

Method note: Direct fetches of mom.gov.sg, startupsg.gov.sg, enterprisesg.gov.sg and acra.gov.sg were blocked by the research environment's egress proxy. Official-page facts below come from search-engine extracts of those pages, cited at their gov URLs. Where only consultancy or vendor sources were available, that is flagged. Anything marked "[secondary]" should be checked against the official page before it is relied on.

## What can a non-Singaporean founding team realistically access?

### Takeaway
A foreign-owned team (under 30% Singapore citizen/PR shareholding) is effectively shut out of most Enterprise Singapore cash grants: Startup SG Founder, Startup SG Tech and EDG/EDGE. The realistic non-dilutive or quasi-equity routes are Startup SG Equity co-investment (via SGInnovate or SG Growth Capital, alongside a qualified VC), the startup tax exemption, IMDA Accreditation@SGD for government procurement, and AI-ecosystem programmes (AI Verify Foundation, AI Trailblazers-type sandboxes). The founder visa route is EntrePass. Joining a government-recognised or internationally renowned accelerator is one of the five ways to qualify for it.

### Cited Findings

**Startup SG Founder (SSG Founder)**
- Run by Enterprise Singapore. Accredited Mentor Partners (AMPs) screen candidates, mentor them and disburse the grant. The grant is S$20k–S$50k, matched 1:1 against founder paid-up capital (one source says the founders co-invest S$10k against a S$50k grant). It is disbursed in tranches over about 12 months — [Raffles CS, secondary](https://rafflescorporateservices.com/?p=5905); [SMU Academy, secondary](https://academy.smu.edu.sg/insights/sme-grants-singapore)
- Older sources cite "up to S$30,000". Treat that figure as stale (pre-2024) — [dollarsandsense, stale](https://dollarsandsense.sg/business/guide-startup-sg-founders-grant-how-entrepreneurs-can-receive-government-support-start-business/)
- Eligibility: first-time founders, full-time commitment, and an innovative, scalable business. Sources say founders must be Singapore citizens or PRs, and one says at least 2 SC/PR first-time founders — [Causo guide, secondary](https://hub.causo.ai/guides/how-to-apply-to-startup-sg-founder-2026); [SUTD (an AMP)](https://sutd.edu.sg/enterprise/venture-innovation-entrepreneurship/startup-sg-founders-grant)
- Sources disagree on the shareholding rule. One says the SC/PR founder must hold at least 30%; another says at least 51% SC/PR ownership is required. No official text could be retrieved to settle it — [SMU Academy](https://academy.smu.edu.sg/insights/sme-grants-singapore); [Raffles CS](https://rafflescorporateservices.com/?p=5905)
- One guide says the company must be a Singapore Pte Ltd under 6 months old — [Raffles CS, secondary](https://rafflescorporateservices.com/?p=5905)
- Example AMPs: NUS, NTUitive, ITE, Antler, Cocoon Capital, Decacorn Capital, Pollinate — [Raffles CS, secondary](https://rafflescorporateservices.com/?p=854)

**Startup SG Tech (POC/POV)**
- The POC cap rose from S$250k to S$400k, and the POV cap from S$500k to S$800k, for applications from 2 Jan 2025. Only one source reports this increase; many pages still list the old caps — [Raffles CS, secondary](https://rafflescorporateservices.com/?p=6016); old caps at [corporateservices.com](https://www.corporateservices.com/singapore/startup-sg-tech/)
- Requires at least 30% local (SC/PR) shareholding — [corporateservices.com](https://www.corporateservices.com/singapore/startup-sg-tech/); [BBCIncorp](https://bbcincorp.com/sg/articles/important-grants-for-tech-startups-in-singapore)
- ESG or its nominee has an option to subscribe for shares worth up to 50% of the grant amount, capped at 49% of the company, at a qualifying equity round. This comes from a single source — [Raffles CS](https://rafflescorporateservices.com/?p=6016)
- MTI parliamentary reply on 10 years of POC/POV award data — [MTI](https://www.mti.gov.sg/newsroom/written-reply-to-pq-on-data-on-startup-sg-tech-proof-of-concept-and-proof-of-value-grant-awards--and-breakdown-by-industry-and-research-institution-origins-in-last-10-years/)

**Startup SG Equity (SSGE)**
- The government co-invests alongside independent, qualified third-party investors in "innovative, Singapore-based technology startups with IP and global market potential". SGInnovate and SEEDS Capital (now SG Growth Capital) are the government-side investors — [SGInnovate](https://sginnovate.com/press-room/sginnovate-appoints-17-co-investors-boost-private-investment-early-stage-deep-tech)
- **March 2026 change:** ESG announced S$1B to top up SSGE and extended support to growth-stage deep-tech startups. SSGE has invested more than S$560M and catalysed more than S$2.6B of private investment. SG Growth Capital and SGInnovate are the contacts for co-investment — [ESG media release Mar 2026](https://www.enterprisesg.gov.sg/-/media/esg/files/media-centre/media-releases/2026/march/mr01026_startup-sg-equity.pdf)
- Feb 2025: SEEDS Capital and Eurazeo committed S$150M over 3 years for Singapore-based deep-tech startups — [FCC Singapore](https://www.fccsingapore.com/news/n/news/eurazeo-and-seeds-capital-collaborate-to-invest-s150-million-in-deep-tech-startups.html)
- One secondary source says SSGE serves companies with under 30% local shareholding (i.e. foreign-owned firms with a Singapore entity) — [Sleek help](https://help.sleek.com/article/qj31ldkxx4-startup-sg-equity-programme)

**Enterprise Development Grant (EDG) / EDGE**
- Requires the company to be registered and operating in Singapore, with at least 30% local SC/PR shareholding. ESG claws back grants when the 30% rule is breached — [Singapore Secretary Services, secondary](https://www.singaporesecretaryservices.com/enterprise-development-grant/); [MTI PQ reply](https://www.mti.gov.sg/newsroom/oral-reply-to-pq-on-ago-report-on-esg/)
- The current SME support level is reported as up to 50%. The earlier 70% enhanced rate ended 31 Mar 2023 — [MTI factsheet, stale](https://www.mti.gov.sg/-/media/MTI/COS-2019/Factsheet_EDG.pdf)
- **Budget 2026 change:** EDG, PSG and MRA are being consolidated into a single scheme called "EDGE", open to all Singapore businesses including non-SMEs. Until it launches, businesses can keep applying via the Business Grants Portal — [ESG Budget 2026](https://enterprisesg.gov.sg/budget-2026)

**SGInnovate**
- A government-owned deep-tech investor: equity investments plus talent and business-building support, with AI a focus area since 2017. It reported 80+ investments and more than S$52M invested by 2020 (stale) — [SGInnovate factsheet 2020](https://www.sginnovate.com/sites/default/files/assets/SGInnovate-Factsheet-28-September-2020.pdf); [Deep Tech Nexus](https://sginnovate.com/press-room/sginnovate-unveils-deep-tech-nexus-strategy-further-strengthen-deep-tech-startup)

**IMDA / AI ecosystem**
- IMDA Accreditation (now Accreditation@SGD) is for Singapore-based companies with an innovative enterprise infocomm product and group revenue under S$100M. Accredited firms get priority consideration in government procurement. A 2026 report mentions a "Greenlane" fast procurement track — [IMDA](https://www.imda.gov.sg/en/Imda/disg/Programmes/2019/04/accreditation); [Bernama 2026](https://bernama.com/en/press/news.php?id=2595688)
- GenAI Sandbox 2.0 (IMDA + ESG) launched Dec 2024 with about 15 GenAI solutions and up to 50% support for SMEs trialling them. Startups take part as **solution providers**, not grant recipients — [Rajah & Tann](https://www.rajahtannasia.com/viewpoints/upcoming-launch-of-genai-sandbox-2-0-for-smes/); [Digital Policy Alert](https://digitalpolicyalert.org/event/24046-launched-genai-sandbox-20-for-smes)
- AI Trailblazers 2.0 (MDDI/IMDA + Google Cloud) aims to help 150 organisations build GenAI solutions in innovation sandboxes — [CXOTV](https://apac.cxotv.news/technology/mci-disg-sng-enterprisesg-and-google-cloud-unveil-new-initiatives-to-advance-singapores-national-ai-strategy-showcase-impactful-gen-ai-solutions-from-ai-trailblazers/)
- AI Verify Foundation is a not-for-profit, wholly owned IMDA subsidiary and open-source community for AI testing and governance, with premier and general membership tiers — [PDPC](https://www.pdpc.gov.sg/news-and-events/announcements/2023/06/launch-of-ai-verify-foundation-to-shape-the-future-of-ai-standards-through-collaboration)
- Budget 2025: Enterprise Compute Initiative (up to S$150M) for businesses to access AI tools and compute via major cloud providers — [Frontier Enterprise](https://www.frontier-enterprise.com/key-enterprise-tech-takeaways-from-singapore-budget-2026/)
- **Budget 2026:**
  - National AI missions in advanced manufacturing, connectivity/logistics, finance and healthcare.
  - A National AI Council chaired by PM Wong.
  - A "Champions of AI" programme.
  - An Enterprise Innovation Scheme 400% tax deduction on qualifying AI spend, capped at S$50k a year for YA2027–2028.
  - Singapore hosts 60+ AI Centres of Excellence.
  - Sources: [Frontier Enterprise](https://www.frontier-enterprise.com/key-enterprise-tech-takeaways-from-singapore-budget-2026/); [The Independent SG](https://www.theindependent.sg/singapore-s-budget-2026-blends-tax-support-with-ai-push-for-long-term-growth); [EDB Budget 2026 insight](https://edb.gov.sg/en/business-insights/insights/singapores-next-growth-chapter-what-international-businesses-should-know-from-budget-2026.html)

**MAS FSTI**
- FSTI 3.0 (Aug 2023) committed up to S$150M over 3 years. Its tracks are Enhanced Centre of Excellence (which extends to corporate VCs that mentor startups), Innovation Acceleration, and ESG FinTech, with a focus on AI and data analytics and on RegTech. Grants go mainly to financial institutions and CVCs, not directly to foreign startups — [MAS](https://www.mas.gov.sg/news/media-releases/2023/mas-commits-up-to-s$150-million-for-technology-and-innovation-in-financial-sector)

**Startup tax exemption (SUTE)**
- Exemption: 75% on the first S$100k of chargeable income, and 50% on the next S$100k, for the first 3 consecutive YAs — [IRAS](https://www.iras.gov.sg/taxes/corporate-income-tax/basics-of-corporate-income-tax/corporate-income-tax-rate-rebates-and-tax-exemption-schemes)
- Conditions:
  - Incorporated in Singapore and a Singapore tax resident.
  - No more than 20 shareholders, all individuals or with at least one individual holding at least 10%.
  - Investment-holding and property-development companies are excluded.
  - Foreign companies and their branches cannot claim it.
  - Source: [IRAS](https://www.iras.gov.sg/taxes/corporate-income-tax/basics-of-corporate-income-tax/corporate-income-tax-rate-rebates-and-tax-exemption-schemes)
- Tax residence requires control and management in Singapore. A team whose board meets abroad may fail the residency test — [IRAS Form C-S notes YA2026](https://www.iras.gov.sg/docs/default-source/uploadedfiles/pdf/explanatory-notes-to-ya-2026-form-c-s.pdf?sfvrsn=1d2386c1_20)

### Inferences
- The SC/PR ownership tests matter more than the paperwork:
  - Founder, Tech and EDG all hinge on SC/PR founders or at least 30% SC/PR shareholding, so a fully foreign Enclave Labs cannot access them unless it adds Singapore citizen or PR co-founders.
  - Adding SC/PR co-founders just to reach 30% would dilute the team and must be genuine. ESG enforces clawbacks.
- SSGE is the most relevant government money for Enclave. It is equity, not a grant, and it needs a qualified lead investor; accelerators that are also SSGE co-investors are the natural path.
- IMDA Accreditation@SGD fits a sovereign, in-VPC enterprise AI product and could open public-sector deals. It needs a Singapore-based entity with substantial local presence.
- FSTI is relevant only if Enclave sells into Singapore financial institutions, which would then be the applicants.

### Gaps
- The official Startup SG Founder eligibility text (SC/PR requirement and 30% vs 51%) could not be retrieved.
- The official Startup SG Tech caps for 2025–2026 and the share-option clause could not be confirmed.
- SSGE co-investment ratios and the treatment of foreign founders are not set out in any source found.
- EDGE launch date and rates: not found.
- AI Verify Foundation membership fees and any startup tier: not found.
- No startup-specific National AI Strategy 2.0 grant was found (e.g. a direct IMDA AI startup grant). Claims of an "AI Accelerate" grant of up to S$250k are unverified.
- No SGInnovate investment data for 2025–2026 was found.

## What does a team need to set up to join a Singapore program (entity, visa, director)?

### Takeaway
A team needs:
- A Singapore Pte Ltd (S$315 ACRA fee, incorporated in hours to days).
- A Singapore-resident director from day one, either a nominee at about S$1.2k–5k a year plus a deposit, or the founder once they hold an EntrePass or EP.
- A company secretary within 6 months and a local registered address.

For the founder, the visa route is an EntrePass: hold at least 30% of the Pte Ltd and meet one of five "innovative" criteria, one of which is backing by a government-recognised or internationally renowned incubator or accelerator. Processing takes about 8 weeks. Tech.Pass is being retired in Jan 2027 in favour of a ONE Pass (AI and Tech) track whose salary bar most early-stage founders won't meet.

### Cited Findings

**ACRA incorporation**
- The ACRA fee is S$315: S$15 for the name application (non-refundable) and S$300 for registration. Incorporation via BizFile+ can take about 15 minutes, or a few hours to a few days if the name or entity is referred — [Left Right Corp, secondary](https://leftrightcorp.com/guides/business-incorporation/singapore-company-incorporation-cost/); [Sleek](https://sleek.com/sg/resources/how-to-register-a-company-with-acra/)
- Requirements:
  - At least one director ordinarily resident in Singapore (Companies Act s145): a citizen, PR, or EntrePass/EP holder.
  - At least one shareholder, which may be foreign.
  - A local registered address.
  - A company secretary within 6 months (s171).
  - Annual return within 7 months of financial year-end (S$60).
  - Changes notified within 14 days.
  - Source: [Left Right Corp](https://leftrightcorp.com/guides/business-incorporation/singapore-company-incorporation-cost/); [DLA Piper](https://www.dlapiperintelligence.com/goingglobal/corporate/singapore/15-director-officer-requirements.html)
- Nominee directors cost about S$1.2k–5k a year, often S$1.8k–3.6k a year, plus a refundable deposit of about S$2k–5k. Example vendor rates are S$2,180/yr with a S$2k deposit, and S$2k/yr with a S$3k deposit. All figures are vendor-sourced — [You.co](https://www.you.co/biz/blog/nominee-director-singapore/); [Singapore Secretary Services](https://singaporesecretaryservices.com/nominee-director-services/); [3E CPA](https://www.3ecpa.com.sg/services/company-setup/nominee-director-service/)
- Since 9 Jun 2025, acting as a nominee director by way of business is reportedly an offence unless arranged through a registered corporate service provider. Single source; verify with ACRA — [You.co](https://www.you.co/biz/blog/nominee-director-singapore/)
- An EntrePass or EP holder can serve as the resident director, which removes the need for a nominee — [Excellence SG](https://www.excellencesg.com/opening-a-business-in-singapore-as-a-foreigner-what-you-need-to-know/)

**EntrePass (MOM)**
- Base requirements:
  - A Pte Ltd registered with ACRA.
  - The applicant holds at least 30% of shares.
  - The company is venture-backed or owns innovative technologies.
  - Excluded sectors include coffee shops, bars, KTV, massage, employment agencies and geomancy.
  - Source: [MOM eligibility](https://www.mom.gov.sg/passes-and-permits/entrepass/eligibility); [MOM FAQ](https://www.mom.gov.sg/faq/entrepass/which-companies-are-considered-venture-backed-or-own-innovative-technologies)
- Applicants must meet at least one innovative criterion — [MOM eligibility](https://www.mom.gov.sg/passes-and-permits/entrepass/eligibility):
  1. At least S$100k raised in a single round from a recognised investor (e.g. SEEDS Capital, Vertex, internationally known VCs, corporates or angels), for any past or current business.
  2. Backing from a government-recognised or internationally renowned incubator or accelerator. MOM's examples are Y Combinator, MassChallenge and Alchemist; consultancy guides add Startup SG Accelerator-recognised programmes and Techstars.
  3. Founded and sold a tech business while managing it.
  4. IP registered with an approved national IP office that gives a significant competitive advantage.
  5. Ongoing research collaboration with a Singapore IHL or research institute. Service contracts don't count.
- Accelerator membership counts as a qualifying criterion on its own (criterion 2), so joining a recognised Singapore accelerator directly helps EntrePass eligibility — [MOM eligibility](https://www.mom.gov.sg/passes-and-permits/entrepass/eligibility); [Singapore Secretary Services](https://www.singaporesecretaryservices.com/singapore-entrepass-2026-eligibility-innovation-criteria-application-guide/)
- No salary or COMPASS test applies. MOM assesses the business's innovation, the founder's track record, and the economic contribution — [Raffles CS, secondary](https://rafflescorporateservices.com/singapore-entrepass-2026-eligibility-compass-application/)
- Validity is up to 1 year for a new pass and the first renewal, then 2 years per renewal. Processing takes about 8 weeks for most applications, and renewal can start 3 months before expiry — [MOM FAQ validity](https://www.mom.gov.sg/faq/entrepass/how-long-is-my-entrepass-valid-for); [MOM renew](https://www.mom.gov.sg/passes-and-permits/entrepass/renew-a-pass)
- Renewal requires meeting thresholds for total business spending and local jobs created. Dependant passes need higher milestones, e.g. more than S$2M raised — [MOM eligibility](https://www.mom.gov.sg/passes-and-permits/entrepass/eligibility); [MOM family passes](https://www.mom.gov.sg/passes-and-permits/entrepass/passes-for-family)
- Only foreigners can apply; SC/PRs are not eligible. The company may be incorporated before applying — [Singapore Secretary Services](https://www.singaporesecretaryservices.com/singapore-entrepass-2026-eligibility-application-guide-requirements-for-foreign-entrepreneurs/)

**Tech.Pass → ONE Pass (AI and Tech)**
- Tech.Pass (EDB) criteria:
  - A last-drawn fixed salary of at least S$22,500 a month (older pages say S$20k).
  - At least 5 years in a leading role at a tech company valued at US$500M or more, or one that has raised US$30M or more, or at a tech VC with US$500M AUM.
  - No employer sponsorship needed.
  - Validity is 2 years, renewable once.
  - Source: [EDB Tech.Pass](https://www.edb.gov.sg/en/incentives-and-programmes/incentives-and-facilitation-programmes/tech-pass/apply-for-a-tech-pass.html); [EDB work passes overview](https://www.edb.gov.sg/en/business-insights/insights/everything-you-need-to-know-about-work-passes-in-singapore.html)
- **2026 change:** At COS on 3 Mar 2026, MOM announced a ONE Pass (AI and Tech) track to replace Tech.Pass. Its terms:
  - Applications open **28 Jan 2027**.
  - EDB stops new and renewal Tech.Pass applications from that date; existing holders keep their pass until it expires.
  - Total monthly income must be at least S$30k, with a fixed salary of at least S$22.5k and the rest from vested ESOP/ESOW.
  - Applicants need at least 5 years as a founder, C-suite or technical lead in tech.
  - The employer must be a tech company, division or VC meeting a US$500M threshold, or US$30M funding for startups.
  - Sources: [EY Sep 2026](https://globaltaxnews.ey.com/news/2026-0583-singapore-announces-launch-of-overseas-and-networks-expertise-pass-ai-and-tech); [Clyde & Co Mar 2026](https://www.clydeco.com/en/insights/2026/03/new-one-pass-ai-and-tech-track-to-be-introduced-in); [Fragomen](https://www.fragomen.com/insights/singapore-new-one-pass-ai-and-tech-track-to-be-available-in-january-2027.html); [MOM ONE Pass eligibility](https://www.mom.gov.sg/passes-and-permits/overseas-networks-expertise-pass/eligibility)
- ONE Pass (standard route):
  - A fixed salary of at least S$30k a month over the past 12 months, or with a prospective Singapore employer.
  - Or a qualifying employer with US$500M market cap or US$200M revenue.
  - Or outstanding achievements in arts, sports or research.
  - Validity is 5 years, and holders can start, operate and work for multiple companies at once.
  - Source: [MOM ONE Pass eligibility](https://www.mom.gov.sg/passes-and-permits/overseas-networks-expertise-pass/eligibility); [EDB](https://www.edb.gov.sg/en/business-insights/insights/everything-you-need-to-know-about-work-passes-in-singapore.html)

### Inferences
- The likely sequence for Enclave Labs:
  1. Incorporate the Pte Ltd with a nominee director and a corporate secretary (about S$315 government fee plus roughly S$2–5k in year-one nominee and secretarial costs and deposit).
  2. Get the accelerator offer letter.
  3. File the EntrePass under the accelerator criterion, or the S$100k-funding criterion if a round has closed. Expect about 8 weeks.
  4. Once the founder's pass is issued, they replace the nominee as resident director.
- Total realistic time from decision to a founder working in Singapore is about 2–3 months.
- ONE Pass and Tech.Pass apply only if a founder already has a senior tech career at a large or well-funded company. Most early-stage founders will use EntrePass.
- EntrePass renewals are tied to local spending and local hiring, so a programme stay of more than a year needs a Singapore hiring plan.

## Any relevant 2025–2026 changes?

### Takeaway
The key changes are:
- An S$1B top-up to Startup SG Equity, extended to growth-stage deep tech (Mar 2026).
- Tech.Pass being replaced by the ONE Pass (AI and Tech) track, with applications from 28 Jan 2027.
- EDG, PSG and MRA being consolidated into "EDGE" (Budget 2026).
- A 400% EIS deduction on AI spend for YA2027–2028.
- A National AI Council and sector AI missions (Budget 2026).
- Reported higher Startup SG Tech caps (2025).
- Tighter nominee-director rules (2025, single source).

### Cited Findings
- SSGE S$1B top-up and growth-stage extension (Mar 2026) — [ESG](https://www.enterprisesg.gov.sg/-/media/esg/files/media-centre/media-releases/2026/march/mr01026_startup-sg-equity.pdf)
- Tech.Pass to be replaced by the ONE Pass (AI and Tech) track (announced Mar 2026; opens 28 Jan 2027) — [EY](https://globaltaxnews.ey.com/news/2026-0583-singapore-announces-launch-of-overseas-and-networks-expertise-pass-ai-and-tech)
- EDGE consolidation of EDG, PSG and MRA — [ESG Budget 2026](https://enterprisesg.gov.sg/budget-2026)
- EIS 400% AI deduction, National AI Council, AI missions and Champions of AI — [Frontier Enterprise](https://www.frontier-enterprise.com/key-enterprise-tech-takeaways-from-singapore-budget-2026/)
- Startup SG Tech caps raised to S$400k (POC) and S$800k (POV) from 2 Jan 2025. Single secondary source — [Raffles CS](https://rafflescorporateservices.com/?p=6016)
- Nominee director offence from 9 Jun 2025. Single secondary source — [You.co](https://www.you.co/biz/blog/nominee-director-singapore/)
- Market Readiness Assistance support raised to 70% for SMEs until 31 Mar 2029 — [ESG Budget 2026](https://enterprisesg.gov.sg/budget-2026)

### Inferences
- The SSGE top-up makes government co-investment the most promising Singapore money for a foreign AI startup in 2026–27, provided a qualified VC or accelerator leads.
- The new 400% EIS AI deduction mainly benefits Enclave's Singapore customers (enterprise AI adoption), which may help sales.

### Gaps
- Whether the EntrePass criteria themselves changed in 2025–2026: no evidence of a change was found. The five-criteria framework appears unchanged, but this could not be confirmed on MOM directly.
- The FSTI status after FSTI 3.0's 3-year window (2023–2026), i.e. any FSTI 4.0: not found. Treat FSTI 3.0 information as possibly stale.
- The NAIS 2.0 startup-specific measures for 2026: not found.
